Numbsters

A solitaire card game. Arrange the hungry Numbsters so one is always eating, and don't let the Mouth reach the top of the stack. Each turn, draw a card, then move or swap one card if you want, then have one Numbster eat another.

        How to play

        Show the rules

        Setup

        The 17 Numbster cards are shuffled into a deck. Six are drawn and shuffled with the Mouth (the 8) to form a stack of 7 cards. The top card of the stack is on the right.

        Each turn

        1. Draw: put 1 card from the deck on top of the stack (skip if the deck is empty).
        2. Move (optional): move 1 card to another position, or swap 2 cards. The Mouth can be moved too.
        3. Eat: one Numbster must eat another. If nothing can be eaten, you lose.

        Basic eating

        If two sequential Numbsters sit on either side of the Mouth, the smaller one eats the bigger one, and the bigger one is discarded. Sequential means the values differ by 1. Seven and Nine also count as sequential, because Eight is the Mouth, not a Numbster.

        Special eating

        Instead of the basic rule, you may use the special rule printed on the top card of the stack. The Mouth must still be directly between the eater and the eaten Numbster, but the card's rule replaces the "sequential" requirement. After eating, the card whose rule you used moves to the bottom of the stack.

        Winning and losing

        You win if the stack is just the Mouth with one Numbster on top of it. You lose if nothing can be eaten, or if the Mouth is on top of the stack at the end of a turn.
